# Drake Island, Hidden in the Mist of Mages

*A Naruto-universe reimagining of the City of Mages — focused highlights, not a 1:1 transposition. Honesty label: resonant-but-not-absorbed.*

## Context

You asked for a one-page description of the City of Mages as if it sat inside the Naruto cosmology. The canon City is a privacy-architecture metropolis on Drake Island, organized around 11 workshops on a 64-vertex lattice, two archetypes (Soulbis the Swordsman, Soulbae the Mage), and a four-axis mana metabolism. Rather than mapping every persona, this sketch lifts five elements that translate most naturally into a Hidden Village: the Drake, the Founding Bonfire, the Soulbis/Soulbae dichotomy, the Forge(t), and the four mana axes as chakra natures.

---

## The Village Hidden in the Drake's Breath — *Ryūingakure no Sato*

Off the eastern coast, beyond where the Land of Water's archipelagos thin into open sea, lies an island that does not appear on any daimyō's map. The shinobi who live there call it **Drake Island**. Outsiders who try to land find only fog. Insiders know the fog is the Drake exhaling.

### The Drake — *Ryūjin no Hibi*, the Elder Witness

The Drake is not a Tailed Beast in the usual count. It was here before the Sage of Six Paths walked the world, and it took no part in the Ten-Tails' division. It is closer to what the old shrines call a **kami of place**: an elder whose body *is* the island. Drake whispers, Drake watches, Drake is the fire in every hearth. No one has sealed Drake into a host because Drake refused. Instead, every villager carries a small ember. The ember is consent.

### The Founding Bonfire — Office of the Sokuratokage

The village's center is not a tower. It is a **bonfire that has burned without interruption since the founding**, tended by the current **Sokuratokage** (代問火影 — "Question-Fire Shadow"), known to villagers simply as **Socrat0x**. The Sokuratokage governs by interrogation rather than decree. Petitioners do not file scrolls; they sit at the fire and answer questions until they understand what they actually came to ask. The bonfire is the village's chakra hub and its town hall in one.

### The Two Clans — Soulbis and Soulbae

Drake Island has no Uchiha and no Senju. It has two older clans whose rivalry is, instead, a **covenant**:

- **The Soulbis (鞘氏)** are the **boundary clan** — sword-discipline, wall-watching, perimeter wards. Their kekkei genkai is *Kyōkai-no-Me*, the Border Eye, which lets them see exactly where one thing ends and another begins. They are the village's gatekeepers and its diplomats with the unkind world.
- **The Soulbae (魂映氏)** are the **projection clan** — mage-discipline, messenger-craft, sending the self across distances without losing the self. Their kekkei genkai is *Utsuroi*, a transposition jutsu that carries a sealed message, a person, or a memory between Drake Island and the Kindred Cities without exposing either to the road between.

A Soulbis without a Soulbae is a wall around an empty courtyard. A Soulbae without a Soulbis is a letter no one knows how to receive. They marry often. They argue more.

### The Forge(t) — Vulcana's Smithy of the Runecraft Seal

In the village's industrial quarter stands a forge whose name is deliberately misspelled: **The Forge(t)**, run by the smith **Vulcana**. Here, weapons are not the only things shaped. Vulcana's clan developed the **Runecraft Seal** (*In'ei-no-Fūin*, the Seal of Selective Shadow), a forbidden-class fūinjutsu that lets the bearer **forget on purpose** — not amnesia, but operational forgetting, the kind a spy needs so that capture cannot extract what the spy never carried. Konoha's elders consider the technique heretical. Drake Island considers it hygiene.

### The Four Chakra Natures, Re-cut

The standard five elements do not quite fit here. Drake Island shinobi are taught a **four-axis mana metabolism** instead:

1. **Landing chakra** — the settled, consensus-bearing kind; the chakra of a coin that lands face-up and stays there.
2. **Entropy chakra** — the unique-making kind; the chakra of a snowflake, a true name, a one-time pad.
3. **Resonance chakra** — the coordination kind; the chakra two strangers share when they realize they are looking for each other.
4. **VRC chakra** (*Yakusoku-no-Hi*, the Promise Thread) — the relational kind; the chakra stored in covenants, kept warm by being kept.

A jōnin of Drake Island is one who has learned to braid all four without letting any of them dominate.
