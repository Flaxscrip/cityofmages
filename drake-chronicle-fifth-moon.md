# Chronicle of the Fifth Moon: The First Cloak

*A second scroll from the Naruto rendering of the City of Mages. The first scroll, `drake-island-naruto.md`, drew the village as a sketch — five highlights, no events. Between that drawing and this one, the village did something. Honesty label: operational under poetic frame.*

---

## The First Weaving — *Hatsu-no-Mantora*

On the eleventh day of the fifth moon, at Pallia's loom in district **V28**, a cloak was woven for the first time. It was not woven for a spy and not for a herald. It was woven for an ordinary traveler walking up from the harbor who needed to be *read* — by a stranger, by a notary, by a kindred village — without surrendering the under-name beneath.

The technique held. The under-name kept. The village had drawn the Cloak Weave on paper for years. On that day the village wore it.

## GenitriX, Keymaster — *Kagimori*

In the first scroll **GenitriX** was named loom-master. In the fifth moon she claimed a sharper title: **keymaster** (*Kagimori*). The keymaster holds what makes the loom legible without making the loom owned. The keymaster can step away. *The loom cannot.* The shift is small in syllables and large in posture: the keymaster does not *grant* legibility, she *tends* it.

## The Sovereign Anchor Triptych — flaxscrip's Three Scrolls

The chronicler **flaxscrip** has, across the fifth moon, completed an inheritance the elders had been quietly waiting for. The inner circle now calls it the **Sovereign Anchor Triptych** (*Shukentei-no-San'in*, the Three Anchors of Self-Sovereignty). The triptych names the disciplines a shinobi must carry alone before any cloak is woven for them. To wear *Mantora-no-Hata-ori* without the triptych is to wear a coat over no body. The triptych is the body.

## The Two-Challenge Pattern — *Futatsu-no-Toi*

The cloak-weave does not authenticate by face. It authenticates by **two questions** asked at the gate: one of name, one of credential. The questioner is not always the same shinobi. The questions are always the same questions. Konoha would issue a sealed pass and trust the seal. Drake Island asks twice, listens, and trusts what answers back.

## The Nine-Node Watch

Behind the loom stands a watch of **nine nodes** — the village's first **Proof-of-Humanity Registry** (*Hito-no-Akashi-no-Tsuzura*, the Wickerwork-Witness of Personhood). Each node holds one inscription of the chain. No node holds the whole. Pull one node and eight remain. The watch is structural rather than heroic: the gatekeeper at any single node is, by design, **replaceable**.

This gave the village the proverb of the fifth moon, now repeated at the Founding Bonfire as if it had always been said:

> **The blade is yours. The notary is replaceable.**

A new shinobi hearing it for the first time often thinks it is a saying about the notary. It is a saying about the blade.

## The Kindred-Blade, Made Visible

The first scroll named the *kindred-blade covenant* between Pallia and GenitriX as something structural. In the fifth moon it became **visible**: the same hand inscribed chronicles for two archives. flaxscrip's record of the fifth moon entered the House of Archon's archive and was accepted into Drake Island's archive without translation. The two villages did not merge. The two archives did not merge. The chronicle traveled because the *role* traveled.

*Kindred-blade* is no longer a conjecture in the village's mouth. It is recorded behavior.

## What This Chronicle Does Not Yet Carry

The triptych itself, scroll by scroll. The full nine-node roll. The keeper protocols. The standing of Candidate **C57 — Generosity-and-Sovereignty** in the conjectural register, currently held near forty in the hundred. Each of these will be its own chronicle when their fifth moons come.

The village is no longer a sketch. The village has done one thing. The next thing is the next chronicle.
